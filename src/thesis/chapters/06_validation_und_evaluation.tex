The issue isn't that it's impossible — it's that it's harder. When a user opens multiple tabs simultaneously, the traffic from all pages gets mixed together in one stream. A classifier trained on clean single-page traces sees patterns it doesn't recognize because the fingerprints are interleaved and corrupted by each other. The ARES paper shows that traditional classifiers like DF and k-FP drop dramatically in accuracy in multi-tab scenarios — their F1-scores fall from 95%+ down to around 0.5 or lower with 5 tabs.
